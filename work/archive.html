60people in the
usability trial

A location app for families. In a 60-person trial most people could complete the core tasks. Verizon later moved on to other priorities.

MINI Takes the States

Verizon × MINI · 2018 · Designer and PM

1week, coast to coast,
every driver a first-time user

Turn-by-turn navigation for a week-long cross-country rally, with every driver a first-time user. When route waypoints failed to trigger in testing, we added adjustable geofence sizes to the admin dashboard. Drivers who joined mid-route could start from the nearest point.

Travelers Haven

Corporate housing · 2018–2019

~30steps to book a rental
before the redesign

Booking a rental took about 30 steps across several internal teams, and background checks ran before availability was confirmed. We moved availability confirmation ahead of the background check and let renters pick properties themselves.

It didn't ship; the company changed direction.
