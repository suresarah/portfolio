Context

Pax8 is a cloud marketplace between managed service providers (MSPs) and software vendors, and it only grows if both sides do. Competitors were launching loyalty programs, and Pax8 had tried one before.

Why the last one failed

The last program was one-sided: only partners earned, and Pax8 funded every reward.

Little to earn, little worth redeeming, and no way to scale. It was discontinued.

What was mine

The business set the model and the tier thresholds.

I owned everything a partner sees and does: their tier, what gets them to the next one, and what changes when they move up, down, in, or out.

Plus the research behind it, and the admin tools Pax8 uses to run it.

Research

We surveyed 72 people at MSPs, including partners who don’t use Pax8, and audited programs like TD Synnex Hyve, Ingram Micro Xvantage, Dell, and Microsoft’s partner incentives.

Partners liked programs that were easy to earn from, easy to spend, and paid out in things that grow the business. They tuned out narrow vendor perks.

Usability test

Two questions: could partners find it, and did the name work?

Caveat: the research panel is about 2% of partner users, so we treated findings as direction, not certainty.

Three decisions

  1. Turn the tier rules into “spend → qualify → earn.” Partners see where they are, what to do next, and what they unlock, not the whole rulebook.
  2. Design for every state, not just the happy path. Welcome period, eligible, approaching, downgraded, and below Ignite, where earning pauses but points can’t look like they’ve vanished.
  3. Make the value clear before asking anyone to join. A pre-opt-in experience to compare tiers, read the terms, and preview rewards. A partner who joins confused becomes a support ticket.

The full case study covers these with the screens, plus keeping point balances accurate when points are money, the admin tools Pax8 runs it with, mobile, and the next iteration.

Launch

Opt-in hit its target in 72 hours. Redemption lagged far behind.

Launched alongside Beyond 2025. Pilot targets: 70% of eligible partners opting in, 10% redeeming a reward.

Looking back

We got people into the program quickly. Looking back, I'd have focused earlier on what happens after enrollment.

Adoption is only the first step. The harder problem is giving people a reason to come back.
